Teacher Toolkit

A shared home for our classroom instruments.

🧩

Lesson Board

Pin several tools on one screen — a noise monitor beside a timer — and swap them without losing what's running.

 🥚

Noise Monitor

Real-time classroom noise level tracker with animated themes.

 🗳️

Quick Poll

Instant feedback from students, no devices required.

 🎡

Random Picker

Spinning wheel name picker with an equity mode.

 ⏱️

Visual Timer

Countdown timer with an animated mascot and fullscreen mode.

 🎯

Zones Check-In

Anonymous emotional check-in — students tap a zone, teacher sees a live class dashboard.

 🎫

Exit Ticket

Three quick end-of-lesson questions — see what stuck before the next class, not after a test.

 🎲

Group Generator

Fast, fair random groups with an animated shuffle and optional pinned seats.

 📋

Behaviour Tracker

One-tap warnings per student, private and predictable, with undo always at hand.

 🔵

Restorative Circle

Guided turn-taking questions for check-ins, conflict resolution, or community building.

 🌱

Growth Mindset

Reframes a negative thought into a growth one, and grows a class "Wall of Yet."

 🔄

Kagan Timer

Runs the class through structured pair/team talk — Think-Pair-Share and more.

 🧩

Choice Board

A 3×3 grid of tasks students pick their own path through, with live class progress.

 🎤

Presentation Timer

Keeps each team's turn and Q&A on time so you can watch, not the clock.

 ✅

Assessment Checklist

Walk the room scoring by rubric instead of gut feel — totals tally themselves.

 ❄️

Snowball Wall

Students build an idea alone, then combine it with a partner, a group of four, and finally the whole class, growing a shared idea wall as they go.

 🌸

Bloom's Question Builder

Type a topic, get ready-to-ask discussion questions at every thinking level, plus cold-call techniques to use with them.

 🃏

Challenge Deck

Students pick a difficulty category and draw a random task card to complete, then draw again — teacher sees live per-student progress.

 🧠

Think Time

A silent countdown before you call on someone, so every student actually has time to think first, then an optional quick pick for who answers.

 🔁

Rotation Timer

A big shared clock and chime for gallery-walk and carousel stations, so every group moves together.

 🌶️

Chili Challenge

Students pick their own task difficulty by chili level, not by "easy/medium/hard" — teacher sees the class's chosen spread live.

 🪜

Help Ladder

A five-step "try this before asking me" display, plus an anonymous "I'm still stuck" signal so you know who still needs you.

 🪙

Coin Flip

A spinning coin picks between two contrasting approaches, or combines random pairs into a fresh creative prompt.

 🧘

Break Timer

A menu of calm sensory and movement breaks, plus optional scheduled micro-breaks on a timer.

 📚

Research

The pedagogical foundation and studies behind each tool.
