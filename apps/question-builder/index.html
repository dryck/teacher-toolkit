    Classroom Questioning Techniques

    Four complementary ways to decide who answers and which question to ask

    🎯

    Individual

    Cold-call one student by name, with no hands raised. Keeps every student mentally engaged because anyone could be picked at any moment, not just the volunteers.

    🔗

    Sequential

    Right after one student answers, immediately call on a second student to build on, add to, or agree/disagree with the first response. Creates a chain of thinking instead of a string of disconnected answers.

    🔁

    Persistent

    If a student says "I don't know," don't let that be the end of it. Simplify or rephrase the question and circle back to that same student until they produce an answer — "I don't know" is never treated as an exit.

    🌱

    Bloom's

    Deliberately choose questions from a specific taxonomy level to match your goal — "Remember" questions for a quick check, "Evaluate" or "Create" questions when you want deeper discussion and original thinking.
